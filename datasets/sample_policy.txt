COMPANY POLICIES - CONFIDENTIAL INTERNAL

1. Remote Work Policy
Employees are allowed to work remotely up to 3 days a week.
All remote work must be approved by the department manager.

2. Expense Policy
Meals during business travel can be expensed up to $50 per day.
Flights must be booked in economy class unless the flight duration exceeds 8 hours.

3. Security Protocol
All employee laptops must be encrypted.
Passwords must be changed every 90 days.
In case of a security incident, contact IT immediately at it-security@enterprise.com.
